About Me

I am currently a senior majoring in Computer Science and minoring in Artificial Intelligence at Cornell University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Claire Cardie. I have also collaborated with Thorsten Joachims and Qi Yang on interactive and human-centered AI, including LLM-based academic peer review and multimodal creativity support.

My research interests lie broadly in natural language processing, machine learning, and reinforcement learning, with a focus on advancing the generalization and adaptability of modern AI systems. I am particularly interested in developing AI systems with reliable reasoning and autonomy, enabling continual self-improvement through interaction, and extending models beyond a single modality to support human-centered applications in real-world domains.

Research

LLM Persona Modeling: Behavioral Alignment in AI-Simulated Personas

Summer 2025 – Present

Advisor: Claire Cardie, Collaborator: Yann Hicke

  • Proposed Joint Training Optimization (JTO), a self-play RL framework that jointly trains paired LLM personas to co-adapt toward a shared behavioral target.
  • Evaluated JTO in minimal doctor–patient simulations to calibrate patient disclosure human annotations.
  • Extending the framework toward multi-turn, domain-agnostic personas for standardized evaluation and IRT-style difficulty estimation.

OmniWizz — Multimodal Music Ideation Assistant

Summer 2025 – Present

Advisors: Qi Yang and Ge Guo

  • Co-led the design and development of an interactive multimodal system that generates coordinated text, image, and audio outputs to inspire music creation.
  • Built the full-stack architecture enabling flexible cross-modal input–output workflows that support diverse and innovative creative exploration. [View Demo]
  • Conducted a 16-participant mixed-methods study, showing OmniWizz expanded ideation, reduced fixation, and preserved creators' sense of ownership.

Automatic Synthetic Data Generation

Spring 2025

Advisor: Claire Cardie

  • Exploring automated dataset construction for self-evolving models with a teacher–student LLM framework that generates domain-specific questions, decomposes tasks, and guides continual adaptation in medicine.
  • Built a hierarchical knowledge map expansion pipeline that covers 226 subfields and produces 2.9K+ teacher–student dialogues and MCQA items without human annotation.
  • Fine-tuned a compact student model, achieving +6.76% accuracy over baseline while using 6.4× fewer training samples than supervised methods.

Culturally and Temporally Contextualized Lyric Generation

Spring 2025

Advisor: Matthew Wilkens

  • Investigating how to adapt large language models for lyric generation that is culturally and temporally grounded.
  • Curated a 31,000-song corpus and fine-tuned LLaMA 3.1 8B with LoRA for context-aware generation.
  • Achieved +3.4% CoSENT, +67.4% ROUGE-2, and +1.6% BERTScore improvements, with human evaluators rating cultural and temporal relevance 36% higher on average.

Automatic Code Generation System

Fall 2024

Advisors: Claire Cardie

  • Extended Aider with a multi-agent system to explore how LLMs can function more effectively as autonomous software engineers.
  • Designed components for task planning, debugging, and progress tracking, orchestrated through an automatic iteration loop, with safeguards including a Git-based rollback mechanism and iterative refinement to preserve best-performing functions.
  • On the Commit-0 Parsel benchmark, the system boosted accuracy by 15.5 percentage points over baseline.

Enhancing Multi-Hop Reasoning in LLMs

Summer 2024

Advisor: Claire Cardie

  • BURE researcher; awarded $7,000 fund for research in Summer 2024.
  • Tackled LLM hallucinations and reasoning errors in multi-hop QA.
  • Developed Reasoning Court, extending ReAct with two retrieval–reasoning agents and an LLM judge capable of selecting or synthesizing factually grounded, coherent answers.
  • Outperformed few-shot prompting baselines on HotpotQA, FEVER, and MuSiQue, achieving up to +7.2% EM and +10.5% F1 improvements while reducing LLM calls compared to hybrid baselines.

Computer Music Synthesis and Composition

Summer 2023

Advisor: Roger B. Dannenberg

  • Explored mapping the digits of π to the minor pentatonic scale, using programming to simulate traditional Chinese instruments for mathematically grounded music.
  • Designed an algorithmic method to generate a melody and an automated countermelody.
  • Composed an original piece and showcased the creative potential of algorithmic music composition.

Algorithms for Big Data

Summer 2021

Advisor: David Woodruff

  • Addressed the high computational cost of GAN-based cartoonization for resource-constrained devices.
  • Sole developer of LWAnimeGAN by combining AnimeGAN and photo2cartoonGAN with architectural optimizations (reduced output channels, depthwise separable convolutions, and removal of HourGlass blocks).
  • Reduced parameters by 8.8%, FLOPs by 56.1%, and inference time by 18.6% while preserving visual quality.
